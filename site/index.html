The SVG is the drawing as shown. The PDF is one page, 35 cm wide, with the same vector lines.

Elevation is the USGS/NGA GMTED2010 30 arc-second grid; outlines are the US Census Bureau's 1:500,000 cartographic boundaries. Both are public domain. The lines are drawn from north to south and each one hides what lies behind it, as on Peter Saville's 1979 sleeve, which reproduced Harold Craft's plot of the pulsar CP 1919.
